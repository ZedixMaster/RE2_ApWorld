# Resident Evil 2

## Where is the options page?

The [player options page for this game](../player-options) contains all the options you need to configure and export a
config file.

## What does randomization do to this game?

Every item pickup in Leon's A scenario is a location. When you pick one up, you send a check instead of getting the
original item. Items for you, from your own world or from other players, are delivered to the item box.

## What is the goal of Resident Evil 2 when randomized?

Reach the ending of Leon's A scenario.

## What does another world's item look like in Resident Evil 2?

Pickups look and play the same as in the original game. Picking one up sends that location's item to its owner.

## When the player receives an item, what happens?

The item is added to the item box. Open any item box to take it out.

## Which version of the game is supported?

Resident Evil 2 (USA), PlayStation, Disc 1 (Leon), serial SLUS-00421, on Normal difficulty.
